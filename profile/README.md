## 中文

LoopsForge（星合易方）为智能体训练与评测构建任务数据。以原创任务、可执行环境与可复核的运行证据，帮助智能体在实际工作场景中把事做成。

我们围绕终端操作、科学工作流、软件开发、浏览器交互、CAD 与 Blender 等方向，按项目需求提供完整任务数据集或专项交付：

- **任务与环境**：原创任务说明、可执行的参考解，以及任务所需的精简运行环境。
- **轨迹与训练数据**：真实执行中的操作、代码、工具调用、工具输出与关键状态变化；验证通过的参考执行轨迹与参考解分别交付。
- **评测与验证**：单独划分评测用途，明确成功标准，配套结果验证器、任务验证与可复核的运行记录。

我们把高校科研积累与 AI 产业工程经验结合起来，重点研究评测方法、执行轨迹分析和评测系统建设。数据坚持原创或合法授权，明确使用范围与训练／评测划分，重视保密和污染风险控制。

任务格式、框架版本、数据接口与交付范围按项目确认，可采用 Terminal-Bench、Harbor 等约定格式。

**合作咨询**：请通过 [sales@loopsforge.ai](mailto:sales@loopsforge.ai) 或[中文联系页](https://loopsforge.ai/zh/contact/)告诉我们任务方向、使用场景与交付目标。

## English

LoopsForge builds task datasets for agent training and separately scoped evaluation. We bring together original tasks, executable environments, and reviewable execution evidence to help agents get work done.

Our work spans terminal operations, scientific workflows, software engineering, browser interaction, CAD, and Blender. Engagements can cover a complete dataset or focused delivery modules:

- **Tasks & environments** — Original task instructions, executable reference solutions, and minimal task-scoped environments.
- **Trajectories & training data** — Actual actions, code, tool calls, tool outputs, and state changes. Verified reference execution trajectories are delivered as distinct assets from reference solutions.
- **Evaluation & validation** — Separately scoped evaluation data, defined success criteria, outcome verifiers, task validation, and reviewable execution records.

Our team combines university research backgrounds with hands-on AI industry engineering. We focus on benchmark methodology, execution-trajectory analysis, and evaluation systems. Original authorship or lawful sourcing, explicit usage rights, confidentiality, and training/evaluation separation guide our work and contamination-risk controls.

Task formats, harness versions, data interfaces, and delivery scope are agreed per project, including Terminal-Bench and Harbor packages where appropriate.

**Work with us:** Share your task domains, intended use, and delivery goals at [sales@loopsforge.ai](mailto:sales@loopsforge.ai) or through our [contact page](https://loopsforge.ai/en/contact/).
